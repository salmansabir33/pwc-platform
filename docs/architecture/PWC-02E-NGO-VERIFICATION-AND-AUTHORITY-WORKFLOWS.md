## 2. Identity, attributes and classification

“Ownership” here means verified institutional control and data stewardship under service terms, not transferring NGO assets/legal ownership to a User. `Organization.id` remains stable; a person owns only their personal account and submissions. Registration status, platform eligibility, mandate, grant, publication and alliance membership are independent facts.

Classification codes below: **P** approved public projection; **O** organization-private; **V** verification-only (assigned case reviewers and authorized submitter for their own material); **R** restricted identity/security evidence. Public means explicit field approval, not permission to expose the backing model.

### Duplicate identity and existing claims

Use Unicode-aware name/alias normalization, locality, authoritative registration tuples and independently corroborated contacts as candidate signals. Preserve original scripts; confusable names and translations need human review. No global unique name/email/domain constraint; no national-ID matching across NGOs. Registration normalization and issuer/jurisdiction namespace must be approved before enforcing uniqueness.

One reviewed active `RegistrationBinding` per `(issuer, jurisdiction, normalized_number)` identifies the canonical subject; shared numbers/branches/reissued numbers must be adjudicated before binding, not forced into an incorrect merge. Unverified submissions may conflict without reserving the authoritative key. Recommend one live equivalent case per applicant/organization/purpose, **not one global claim per NGO**: rival applicants must be able to present segregated cases. Rate limits and case linking reduce abuse, not first-submitter ownership.

A public match offers only already-public data and a claim entry point. Nonpublic matches produce a neutral “manual review required” result; no hidden organization existence/contact/reviewer details. New drafts remain application data until deduplication resolves the target. A claim against an existing UUID never creates a parallel operational tenant or reveals its contents.

Initial merge recommendation: no operational data merge. Record a reviewed duplicate relation/public canonical alias without private-ID redirects; retain original tenant/evidence IDs and deny grants on aliases. Actual consolidation requires separate approved migration, both legitimate institutional consents or legally validated resolution, tenant-by-tenant data inventory, preservation/rollback and regrant plan. No bulk FK reassignment, union of roles or evidence disclosure. Substantive leadership/title disputes go to appropriate NGO governance/legal resolution; PWC determines software access under its approved terms, not who legally owns an NGO.

## 3. Separate lifecycle machines

All transitions require expected revision, authorized actor, reason, UTC time and append-only event. Unlisted transitions deny. Expiry is checked from the database wall clock on access; a delayed cleanup job cannot prolong authority.

### Required cross-machine synchronization

The separate machines must not drift. Under the same organization authority-root transaction, recompute eligibility, increment its revision and record the corresponding workspace/publication fence:

- New unverified/rejected applications remain provisional, or pending during review, with own-application access only. Case approval alone leaves the workspace pending until exact recipient activation succeeds.
- Current organization verification plus valid mandate/appointment/recipient/grant may produce workspace `verified` only through the approved activation writer. Publication still requires its independent gate, including for a verified but unpublished workspace; the existing public queryset must be integrated with that gate in PWC-04D before real activation.
- Material revalidation of an active subject, suspension, revoked eligibility or an organization-wide credible authority dispute makes the workspace restricted and withdraws publication. For a safely isolated mandate dispute, fence that mandate and all its dependent grants; other independent valid mandates may continue only if the approved triage policy explicitly permits it. No broad “verified” status overrides a restricted source.
- Restoration of eligibility does not restore revoked/disputed authority, publication consent or old activation controls. Require fresh exact activation/regrant authorization for previously restricted operational authority and renewed publication approval where withdrawn. An expired verification never gains a grace period because a workspace projection has not yet been updated.
- Software exit uses deactivated workspace state, separately from alliance withdrawal. Missing/inconsistent projections deny; authorize from primary verification/mandate/root facts, not an eventually synchronized UI status. Reconciliation jobs may repair projections but cannot mint authority.

`rejected` means that case failed, not permanent proof of fraud. `suspended` is a protective restriction pending review; `revoked` invalidates previous eligibility/authority. A verified organization may have zero valid representatives, and a verified representative may have no active technical grants. Workspace active requires current organization verification **and** valid source mandate/appointment, recipient acceptance, eligible User/session, pinned grant and approved policy. No alternate field overrides a denied state.

Material revalidation triggers: changed legal identity/registration status/jurisdiction, governance/representative mandate, disputed control, contradictory/forged evidence, reported dissolution, loss of trusted corroboration or validity deadline. Cosmetic typo/public-description edits need normal authorized review, not automatic authority loss. Proposed material changes are drafts, never overwrite trusted identity or extend expiry; credible invalidation restricts immediately under approved triage. No grace period for authority-dependent access; service-continuity periods need separate approval and cannot override expired/revoked authority.

## 4. Actors and decision powers

| Actor                                                         | May do, if explicitly authorized                                                                                      | Cannot obtain by this relationship alone                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Personal User / contact controller / creator/founder claimant | Submit own application, own evidence and appeal                                                                       | Existing NGO records, admin, representative, finance or publication                                    |
| Verified legal/designated representative                      | Exercise explicitly verified mandate; approve appointments only where mandate and organization policy allow           | Automatic admin, finance/signatory or alliance vote                                                    |
| Appointed administrator                                       | Exact operational bundle; appointments only with separately approved appointment-management power and source decision | Root mandate issuance, finance, legal commitment or other NGO access                                   |
| Member / volunteer                                            | Separate approved membership/volunteer activities                                                                     | Administration, representation, finance or PWC roles                                                   |
| Finance operator / financial signatory                        | Separately approved finance actions / independently evidenced financial mandate                                       | Each other's powers, general legal commitments or NGO administration                                   |
| PWC alliance representative / Board/committee member          | Approved alliance/committee context                                                                                   | Verification-review authority or NGO-private data                                                      |
| Verification assessor / approving reviewer / appeal reviewer  | Assigned case fields/evidence and review decisions under separate IAM; reviewer conflicts checked                     | Operational tenant reads, arbitrary leadership selection, grant issuance outside case-bound activation |
| Safety responder (proposal E13 only)                          | Exact approved service restriction after triage; no data browsing                                                     | Universal support/export, mandate replacement or finance recovery                                      |

No user can approve their own bootstrap, benefit as an approver on their own appointment or satisfy independent review through a second account. Reviewer-to-NGO relationship/conflicts must be disclosed and checked. Lack of objective independent-review capacity blocks the decision. An NGO may evidence external/offline governance without every signatory having an app account; corroboration must establish distinct humans and authority without mass account creation.

## 5. Twelve complete workflows

### Common contract applying to every workflow

The entries below inherit all of the following (not optional): authenticated current personal session with appropriate clear hold for authority actions; explicit application/case scope or NGO path; no tenant authority from supplied IDs; purpose-specific permissions; rate/body limits; exact state/revision preconditions; immutable evidence versions; actor/process distinction; transaction and assurance rules in §8. Applicants can use the non-operational case channel after loss of NGO access, but it grants no operations or protected command bypass. A held account cannot activate or change authority; sensitive evidence access remains restricted under the approved intake policy E10.

**Audit:** record attempted command/result, case/organization scope, actor/session or approved process, decision/approval/evidence references, policy and revisions, correlation and time; no raw documents/secrets. Success/transition/audit/outbox are atomic. Redacted denials are attempted after rollback, not guaranteed durable during outage. **Notifications:** after commit only, generic authenticated inbox/deep link; no documents, tokens, allegations or private NGO content in email/push. Authenticate and authorize retrieval; do not notify a rival claimant of another case's existence. **Failure:** no success on uncertain commit; owned idempotent status query, unchanged authority on failed mutation, no partial activation. **Privacy:** per-field/case-partition checks, no public case enumeration, no raw provider URLs/log payloads, reviewer minimum-data scope.

### W01 — Create a new NGO profile

- **Initiator / preconditions:** verified personal account, explicit create intent, no NGO grant required; own current draft only.
- **Evidence:** minimum draft fields (§2), then appropriate-track identity/appointment evidence before submission; creator assertion is not proof.
- **Decision / transitions:** applicant saves draft; dedup triage selects new subject versus claim. Proposed new Organization and provisional workspace are created only after safe identity resolution; no admin grant. Draft → submitted only with required submission fields.
- **Allowed actions:** edit own application, supply evidence, withdraw, see own sanitized status; no roster/import/finance/team invites/verified publication.
- **Audit / notices:** application creation, version, candidate-review outcome and submission receipt to applicant; no hidden matches disclosed.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** duplicate race leaves reviewable application, not a second active binding; neutral match error, spam controls, no third-party personal/beneficiary data required.

### W02 — Request control of an existing NGO

- **Initiator / preconditions:** personal User addressing an approved public UUID or neutral manual-match flow; cannot read private workspace.
- **Evidence:** identity of proposed representative, reason for claim, current organizational mandate and independently corroborated contact/registration or informal evidence.
- **Decision / transitions:** reviewer links segregated case to existing institution; submitted → in_review/needs_information/disputed. No first-claim priority or workspace reassignment.
- **Allowed actions:** own submissions/status only; current legitimate holders retain independent rights unless credible risk triage restricts them.
- **Audit / notices:** link, conflicts and triage decisions; safely notify established authorities of credible control challenge after safety review, without exposing adversary evidence/contact. Mere spam claims do not create alerts exposing relationships.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** foreign/absent private cases indistinguishable; conflicting claims segregated; no grant on name/contact similarity; triage cannot disclose current admin roster to claimant.

### W03 — Establish first legitimate representative

- **Initiator / preconditions:** applicant in W01/W02; no existing admin needed; track and target identity resolved.
- **Evidence:** organization eligibility dossier plus current governance instrument/resolution/designation with exact scope, term and signatories; informal equivalent and external corroboration per E01/E03. Verify authority behind the signature, not just the signature image.
- **Decision / transitions:** distinct qualified assessor and independent approver validate same immutable dossier. Mandate claimed → under_review → verified; disputed/rejected if insufficient. Organization eligibility decision remains separate.
- **Allowed actions:** case review only; verified mandate alone creates no operational grant. Reviewer cannot substitute their preferred recipient.
- **Audit / notices:** corroboration method/source/time, conflicts, both reviewer appointments, mandate validity and recipient acknowledgement; applicant gets redacted outcome.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** absent quorum, unverifiable governance or conflicts blocks. Two accounts are not two humans. Platform reviewer IAM bootstrap is separate E04; no circular NGO-admin requirement or synthetic shortcut.

### W04 — Submit verification evidence

- **Initiator / preconditions:** applicant or separately authorized case contributor; owns exact submission partition, open case.
- **Evidence:** category checklist in §6 and versioned rubric; each item declares provenance/authority being asserted.
- **Decision / transitions:** upload session → quarantine → scanned/validated → accepted-for-review or rejected-upload. Complete manifest submission freezes that evidence version; case draft/needs_information → submitted.
- **Allowed actions:** upload/replace own draft before submission, respond to specific information request; no overwriting reviewed evidence or inspecting opponent documents.
- **Audit / notices:** manifest digest, storage version, submitter, scan outcome and receipt; no document filename in external notification.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** reject oversized/polyglot/archive-bomb/unsupported content; scan failure stays quarantine, abandoned uploads disposed per schedule; uploaded bytes and possession prove nothing about authority.

### W05 — Review evidence

- **Initiator / preconditions:** reviewer with current case assignment, clear eligible authentication/assurance and nonconflict attestation; evidence passed quarantine.
- **Evidence:** frozen submission, authoritative issuer checks, independent callback/site/other permitted corroboration, provenance and contradictions. A callback to applicant-supplied number alone is not independent verification.
- **Decision / transitions:** assessor accepts assignment; submitted → in_review → needs_information or recommendation; independent approver receives same revision. Neither can edit applicant evidence.
- **Allowed actions:** view authorized case fields, request minimized information, append findings; no operational records/finance/public document URLs.
- **Audit / notices:** reads/downloads, source check and rationale, reassignment/conflicts; applicant receives information request, not confidential fraud heuristics.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** reviewer authority expiry/revocation blocks further access, including downloads; forged/mismatched evidence escalates to segregated dispute/risk review; no officer override of quorum.

### W06 — Approve or reject verification

- **Initiator / preconditions:** independent approving reviewer after W05; frozen complete dossier, valid assessor appointment, no conflict, exact policy/revisions.
- **Evidence:** reasoned assessor recommendation and corroboration; approve organization eligibility and representative mandate as distinct decisions with distinct scopes/validity.
- **Decision / transitions:** case in_review → approved/rejected; organization pending → verified/rejected as appropriate, mandate decision separately. Eligibility approval alone does not set workspace operationally verified or publish it.
- **Allowed actions:** record reviewed outcome; generate exact bounded activation authorization only when all bootstrap conditions are approved. No ad hoc grants/bundle expansion.
- **Audit / notices:** both decisions, approvers, rubric, expiry and reason; redacted rejection and appeal channel to applicant; no public allegation of fraud.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** changed evidence invalidates pending approvals; tie/conflict → disputed, not approved; authority/revision/audit failure rolls back outcome.

### W07 — Activate organization workspace

- **Initiator / preconditions:** exact approved recipient accepts single-use activation authorization; active eligible account/session, mandated assurance, current org/mandate/approval, no restriction/dispute. Publication integration approved before changing real workspace state (§3).
- **Evidence:** W06 decisions, recipient acknowledgement, approved root policy and exact initial nonfinance grant specification.
- **Decision / transitions:** narrow authorized activation service revalidates all prerequisites under locks, consumes authorization, creates provenance/root grant, advances authority revision and workspace state in one transaction. Existing synthetic state is never converted.
- **Allowed actions:** after commit, only approved initial operational/appointment actions; no finance/member/volunteer/alliance/signatory auto-creation.
- **Audit / notices:** immutable activation consumption and grant/source decision; recipient and independently established organizational contacts notified through safe channels.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** expired/replayed/other-user token, changed mandate/policy/recipient, hold or conflicting activation denies; unique consumption enforces one result. Lost response uses owned status, never mint a second root grant.

### W08 — Invite additional authorized administrators

- **Initiator / preconditions:** currently authorized appointment manager, current root mandate, explicit NGO policy approval; personal admin title alone insufficient.
- **Evidence:** organizational appointment decision naming exact recipient, nonfinance bundle/scope/term and quorum; verified intended recipient User acceptance before grant. Unregistered invitee must complete personal identity first; no automatic User creation.
- **Decision / transitions:** proposal → independent required approval(s) → invited → accepted/awaiting_activation → active grant after all checks. No self-benefiting approval, invitation-role editing or replacement of acceptance with email delivery.
- **Allowed actions:** only subset allowed by the exact issuer-command-target matrix and mandate. Recipient powers start only after successful activation.
- **Audit / notices:** proposal, approval, hashed invite lifecycle, acceptance, grant issuance; current authorized representative(s) and recipient informed without roster disclosure.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** token expires/revokes on source change, revoke, policy revision or recipient mismatch; stale queued invite cannot escape issuer/mandate checks. Approval proves mandate, not membership or finance.

### W09 — Transfer, replace or revoke authority

- **Initiator / preconditions:** appropriately empowered representative/appointment manager for their permitted target; independent review required for root mandate replacement, self-benefit or disputed removal. Compromise can use approved restrict-only safety path.
- **Evidence:** current/new organizational decision, exact target grant/mandate, reason and expected revisions; verified replacement identity and acceptance for planned departure.
- **Decision / transitions:** ordinary voluntary last-admin departure waits for accepted legitimate successor; root change W03/W06 then atomic new authority and terminal old-source/dependent revocation. Urgent compromise revocation never waits for successor. Planned transfer is not editing a grant's subject.
- **Allowed actions:** scoped terminal revoke/replace only; no automatic finance/signatory reassignment, no automatic revocation of unrelated independent mandates. A disputed target cannot be removed by a self-interested rival acting alone.
- **Audit / notices:** before/after revisions, source/dependent changes, approvers and reason; established affected authorities notified with safety exceptions recorded.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** race with invite/renewal revalidates root; revoke-first denies new authority. Audit failure means no claimed successful revocation; initiate approved incident containment, not a silent success. Exact commit semantics in §8 apply.

### W10 — Appeal rejected or disputed claim

- **Initiator / preconditions:** original applicant or legitimately identified affected party in their own case channel; open appeal window/exception rules require E12, not invented statutory deadlines.
- **Evidence:** challenged decision reference plus new/corrected evidence or procedural grounds; no right to opponent's raw file.
- **Decision / transitions:** new linked appeal case assigned to independent reviewer not party to original approval chain; prior outcome immutable. Upheld/overturned/remitted decision references reasons; successful appeal still requires fresh W03/W06/W07 checks, not grant resurrection.
- **Allowed actions:** own evidence and redacted reason/status; no operations during appeal merely because it is pending.
- **Audit / notices:** appeal submission/assignment/outcome and redaction decisions; only appropriate parties informed.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** threats, forged papers or unavailable independent adjudication remain restricted/pending; substantive legal leadership conflict referred outside PWC adjudication. Review timetable is operational approval, not an access bypass.

### W11 — Recover organizational control after departure

- **Initiator / preconditions:** evidenced representative/new candidate through segregated recovery case; no currently usable administrator is required.
- **Evidence:** current governance/appointment, previous mandate/history where lawfully accessible to reviewer, independent corroboration and explanation of departure/compromise. Password reset/contact ownership is never sufficient.
- **Decision / transitions:** freeze affected authority if credible; independent W03/W06 review; revoke obsolete roots/dependents, issue new bounded activation authorization, require W07 acceptance. Do not reactivate old tokens/grants even if original user returns.
- **Allowed actions:** case-only pending recovery. Reviewers see necessary provenance, not roster or finance to “prove” control.
- **Audit / notices:** applicant, reviewer conflict/quorum, recovery basis, old/new source links, preexisting contact notices and documented safe-notice exceptions.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** no legitimate corroboration/approver means no takeover; preserve institution/data in restricted mode. Missing all users is an operational recovery dependency, not permission for unilateral Board/staff appointment.

### W12 — Suspend compromised or fraudulent organization

- **Initiator / preconditions:** report by any permitted channel; only separately approved, currently authorized safety responder can apply restriction after credible triage. A report itself confers no power and causes no automatic freeze.
- **Evidence:** bounded risk indicators and triage record; recommend single authorized responder may restrict-only for imminent harm, with independent prompt review due under E13. No ability to grant/read through this emergency role.
- **Decision / transitions:** active/verified → suspended/restricted and authority revision fence; revoke compromised sources as justified. Review deadline triggers escalation, **not automatic unfreeze**. Fraud finding/revocation requires independent reviewed decision; remediation uses pending/new verification and regrant.
- **Allowed actions:** stop unsafe activity, preserve evidence; maintain separately scoped appeal/recovery channel. No support browse, finance intervention or selecting new leadership.
- **Audit / notices:** trigger, affected scope, responder authority, reviewer/deadline and incident link; safe notices to established contacts, delayed only by approved safety/legal basis and recorded review.
- **Failure / security / privacy:** restriction failure returns unavailable, never success; activate approved service-level containment if necessary. Reviewer mistake/malice is independently reviewable; no deleting cases/history to conceal it.

## 6. Evidence and privacy architecture

| Evidence category                                        | Purpose / corroboration                                                                                                        | Minimum-data control                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Registration/constitution/trust or incorporation records | Establish claimed subject, issuer/jurisdiction and governance; authoritative source check where available                      | Relevant extracts/redactions; no blanket assertion that one Pakistani statute/document applies to every NGO.                                                             |
| Resolution/designation/appointment and term              | Establish representative, exact appointment powers and validity; check approving body's legitimacy and independent signatories | Capture relevant decision, not whole meeting/member roster. Distinguish legal representative from financial signatory.                                                   |
| Informal activity/governance evidence                    | Credible group existence, welfare purpose, decision process and current designation; independent references/corroboration      | No mandatory beneficiary photographs, medical records, donor bank statements or invented registration. E01 defines sufficient alternatives.                              |
| Representative identity/contact binding                  | Bind human to appointment and User; resist account substitution/duplicate-human quorum                                         | Avoid full national-ID copies by default; if exceptionally necessary, require approved necessity, legal basis and restricted redacted handling. Never public/searchable. |
| Dispute/recovery materials                               | Resolve software-access decision and current mandate; independently sourced counter-evidence                                   | Submission partitions, restricted conflict notes and redacted reasons; protection against retaliation.                                                                   |
| Verification attestation/provenance                      | Record what was checked, by whom, when, under what policy and until when                                                       | Minimized structured result with immutable references; digest proves byte identity, not authenticity or consent.                                                         |

Storage specification: provider undecided; private encrypted objects and backups; opaque unguessable keys, tenant/case/partition metadata, authenticated TLS upload, declared-size/type limits, MIME/content verification and malware/quarantine pipeline. No external URL fetch from arbitrary applicant URLs (SSRF); independently controlled external checks need safe egress and review. Strip metadata/render safely; reject dangerous active content. Documents are immutable versions; never execute uploaded material or trust scan success as proof of legitimacy.

Upload sessions authorize one actor/case/category/version and bounded expiry/size, never a public read capability. Final manifest verifies object identity/checksum and scan outcome. Interrupted uploads are not evidence accepted for review. No lock across object-store/registry/provider calls. Review/finalize uses immutable checked references and handles TOCTOU/substitution by version/checksum match.

Recommend backend-mediated downloads with fresh primary authorization, case assignment, hold/state/revision checks and durable read audit before bytes; attachment/no-store/no-referrer/nosniff, sanitized filenames, no inline active documents, no token-bearing query string. No long-lived presigned GETs. E07 must approve limits and any bounded streaming revalidation window: a stream already authorized before revocation can deliver bytes already sent; downloaded copies/screenshots cannot be recalled. Close new requests immediately after revoke and fence remaining chunks where feasible. Do not claim absolute revocation of delivered bytes.

Retention specification requiring E06: each class has approved purpose/legal basis, controller/processor responsibility, notice, access role, retention start event, duration, deletion method, hold exception, backup purge lag and reviewer. Draft/abandoned uploads, rejected/appealed raw evidence, verified raw evidence, restricted identity extracts, mandate/approval attestations, audit/outbox records and backups each need a schedule. No numeric legal retention claim is made. Deletion jobs recheck case/appeal/legal hold and version; audit disposition without raw content. Legal holds require separately authorized reason/scope/review; a normal reviewer cannot indefinitely retain material by toggling a flag. Backup restoration reapplies deletion tombstones and authority revocations before traffic. If mandatory provenance is no longer valid/available, deny renewal/activation; deletion never silently recreates a mandate. Real intake blocked until policy and storage controls are accepted.

Expiry itself always denies even before terminalization job.

Changes to material payload/revisions invalidate approvals and intent. - Runtime must lack direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE on root/grant/mandate/approval/audit authority tables and cannot own functions/tables or inherit migration/fixture credentials. ## 8. Transactions, provenance and concurrency

### Required provenance paths

| Result                                | Only proposed legitimate source                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial representative mandate        | NGO's independently corroborated governance designation → assigned assessor + independent approving reviewer → immutable mandate decision               |
| Initial operational/appointment grant | Current organization decision + verified mandate + accepted exact activation authorization + approved assurance → narrow audited activation writer      |
| Additional administrator              | Current mandate/organization policy + explicit eligible appointment manager + required independent NGO approvals + recipient acceptance → narrow writer |
| Renewal/replacement                   | New current source validation, fresh exact approvals/assurance and bounded validity → new grant, terminal old grant; never in-place expiry extension    |
| Temporary delegate (disabled)         | Later approved exact one-hop live parent subset, explicit acceptance and expiry; root/parent revocation denies derivative access                        |
| Recovery grant                        | New independent case/mandate validation + new activation; never personal recovery, legacy role, Board, staff or fixture inheritance                     |
| Reviewer appointment                  | Separately approved platform IAM appointment with independent approver and restricted provisioning provenance; never self-registration or NGO authority |

A completed review decision records that its reviewers were eligible and nonconflicted **at the decision transaction**. Ordinary later departure/expiry of a reviewer appointment does not erase valid historical decisions or require the reviewer to remain logged in for every NGO access. New assessments/decisions require current reviewer authority; pending approvals/activation controls are rechecked for cancellation, compromise flags and exact validity/revisions. A credible reviewer-compromise finding fences affected decisions/mandates through approved incident triage and requires independent re-review; it cannot silently rewrite past evidence. Recipient activation authenticates the recipient's current session, not the reviewers' historical sessions.

Finance/signatory/alliance/support grants have **no issuance path in this package**. They require separate domain approvals. A reviewer decision is not a generic grant-management entitlement.

**Transaction protocol proposal:** preserve ADR-0001/0002 prefix exactly: locate untrusted selectors without releasing data → begin primary READ COMMITTED transaction → lock all involved Users in stable UUID order (actor, recipient, reviewer/approver/issuer identities whose state is relied on) → contact/factor rows stable order → AuthSessions → challenge/credential/intent rows in existing helper order → relevant platform reviewer-authority roots in stable order → NGO authority roots in Organization UUID order → mandate/grant rows in stable UUID order → case/approval/domain rows → append audit/outbox/receipt. No late User/auth locks, lazy serializer queries, external I/O or replica decisions. Existing synthetic reads keep their own approved root path; do not make them real by adding this document's roots.

The platform-root prefix is a proposed extension, not a claim about current helpers. GM10 review must inventory every writer/reviewer lifecycle and auth call graph to confirm one global order and prevent reviewer-revoke/NGO-command deadlocks. A selector set that changes under lock causes rollback/restart with the complete set; never acquire an earlier-class lock late. Revocation of any relied-on source must serialize through the same affected root protocol. Cross-NGO bulk authority commands are excluded initially; no independent transactions pretending to be one atomic transfer.

Within locks reauthenticate transport/current credential/session, User/hold/epoch/recovery generation/factor state; re-read org eligibility/restriction, reviewer authority/conflicts, mandate/appointment, policy versions, exact command mapping and approvals. Authorize tenant/case entry before disclosing revisions/conflicts. Then validate expected revisions and server-canonical intent digest. Bind actor/session/NGO/recipient/source/target/bundle/scope/validity/evidence manifest/policy/revisions; consume approved operation-specific assurance and approval/activation controls atomically with mutation, terminal old grants if needed, increment root/target revisions, and write immutable audit/outbox/receipt. Final database wall-clock check after audit and before commit enforces every deadline. No union of one source's scope with another source's limits to satisfy a command.

Assurance recommendation for authority issuance, activation, transfer, revocation, policy changes and review approval: current authenticated eligible session, clear hold, operation-bound password/TOTP proof as accepted by GM06/GM07; exact recent-TOTP reuse and factor-retention semantics must match ADR-0001. No recovery-code/newly enrolled-factor bypass. First bootstrap may use a newly created legitimate account with normally enrolled MFA **only through the independently verified identity/mandate binding and an explicitly approved bootstrap assurance rule**, not by clearing a restricted recovery session. Missing nonpersonal mapping denies; do not append strings to `accounts.mfa.OPERATIONS` as a substitute for this integration.

Recommended HTTP preconditions: required `If-Match`/expected revisions (428 missing, 412 stale), authorized lifecycle conflicts 409, invalid proof 403, unavailable lock/audit/DB dependencies 503. Before entry authorization, unknown/foreign resources share redacted 404. Reconcile exact error envelopes and intent invalidation/abuse evidence with existing GM08/auth semantics before implementation; these are proposed new command semantics, not changes to current synthetic read contracts.

Commands require scoped idempotency keys: `(actor, logical session, organization/case, command, key)` plus canonical payload digest. Same authorized successful retry returns minimized prior outcome without issuing authority again; changed body conflicts; status retrieval reauthorizes ownership/current visibility and never replays secrets. Retention/time window and response-loss behavior require E14/GM08 approval. If commit status is uncertain, return unavailable and reconcile owned receipt; do not blindly retry root issuance or assert rollback. Semantic conflict invalidates exact intent; transient rollback handling must preserve required failure/invalidation evidence in a reviewed failure transaction. No promise that a rolled-back audit entry is durable.

Revocation linearization: transaction holding current root may serialize a bounded read/command before revoke. Once revoke commits, new authorization, downloads, jobs and notification retrieval depending on that source deny. Already serialized/delivered bytes cannot be recalled. Bump revisions and clear client private state; capability snapshots are not authority. Parent/mandate dependency is checked on every derivative access, not by eventual asynchronous cascade. Planned last-admin transfer serializes on root so two departures cannot both pass; emergency revoke can leave zero valid admins and returns to W11. If revocation write/audit fails, do not report success: fail closed for that operation, alert and use separately approved service containment. No transaction can guarantee mutation when the primary DB is unavailable.
