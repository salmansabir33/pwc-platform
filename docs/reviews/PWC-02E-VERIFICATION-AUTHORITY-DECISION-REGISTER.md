### 8.3 Incomplete choices and apparent conflicts — not silently ratified

1. **E05 numeric bounds are documented, not newly invented.** Adopt them only as product upper bounds; exact calendar/deadline/renewal rules still require joint review. The real grant ceiling is not permission to extend synthetic 24-hour rows or auth proof/session limits. No retention, safety-review, appeal, rate-limit or streaming duration can be borrowed from these unrelated numbers.
2. **E03 two platform reviewers versus E09/GM09 unspecified NGO quorum:** different authorities/purposes. The documented assessor/approver product choice does not decide how many NGO governance signatories appoint an administrator, prove distinct humans, or create a financial quorum. E13's one restrict-only responder is a different, still-gated safety role, not a one-person grant/claim approval.
3. **GM01 exact command charter is incomplete.** Synthetic-first/exclusions are selected, while “preferably a smaller issue/revoke subset” is not an exact allow/deny list. GM02 has no approved action atoms, bundle versions or issuer-target matrix; GM03 has no complete synthetic issuer provisioning contract. Do not manufacture these from current reader/admin-probe bundles.
4. **GM06/E10 contain an explicit unresolved fork** (block factor removal versus protected-command unavailability), plus missing operation-specific assurance, factor continuity, recent-TOTP and bootstrap/held-account rules. Endorse the safeguards, not either unchosen branch. Initial legitimate account bootstrap and recovery hold bypass are distinct; no new-factor loophole or impossible ban on all new accounts is adopted.
5. **E11/GM04 independent appointments versus delegation:** mandate-backed appointments are not arbitrary derivative chains; issuer eligibility at issuance/provenance is distinct from a delegate's continuing live-parent dependency. Exact source classification, mandate invalidation/cascade and compromise review need joint NGO/security/database approval. Ordinary departure must not erase valid independent appointments; revoking their mandate must block dependent authority.
6. **E15 delegation direction is scoped, not enabled.** Initial nondelegating/synthetic slices and later one-hop product direction coexist. No multihop, self-escalation, root/finance/signatory or implicit grant-management delegation is authorized. E13 safety/legal and E16 finance/alliance/support are not newly conferred powers.
7. **Revocation is immediate at the authorized committed transition**, as in ADR-0002: no grace or positive authorization cache for new dependent actions, and no last-admin exemption for compromise. A previously serialized/delivered response cannot be recalled; a failed/uncertain DB write cannot be reported as a successful revoke. Neither asynchronous cleanup nor restoration may resurrect revoked authority. The required writer/locks/proof/audit contract remains gated; no production revocation feature is claimed to exist.
8. **Other incomplete contracts:** evidence sufficiency/registration namespaces; reviewer appointing root/conflicts; disclosure/consent and current public-queryset integration; technical writer and global lock graph; schemas/proof failure/receipt semantics; legal basis/retention; storage budgets/revocation windows; triage/escalation/appeal and safe-notice rules are not fully specified. Requirements and responsible functions remain in the matrix/original rows. No contradiction with the approved deny-first architecture is resolved by weakening it.
