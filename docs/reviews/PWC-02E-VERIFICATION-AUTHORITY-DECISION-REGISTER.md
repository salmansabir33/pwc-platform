## 2. Decision classification and approval protocol

All **E01–E18 below are UNRESOLVED**. “Recommend” is an option, never an acceptance. Product-owner review cannot substitute for security or legal approval in their domains.

Gate legend:

- **I:** blocks implementation of the affected policy-dependent transition/command/constraint (not unrelated read-only design or separately authorized default-off scaffolding).
- **A:** blocks authority activation, including any real mandate/root/grant or real reviewer permission.
- **U:** blocks intake/access to real verification documents and personal data.
- **R:** blocks production release/operation.
- **F:** safely deferred feature; prohibited until separately approved, does not block initial nonfinance/no-delegation design.

Every entry needs a dated record naming accepted option, accountable human owner/reviewer, exact scope/version/environment, evidence/test criteria, remaining exclusions and references to updated contracts. Reviewer functions below must be assigned actual accountable humans by the owner before execution. Partial approval closes only its recorded scope. No approval record is present.
